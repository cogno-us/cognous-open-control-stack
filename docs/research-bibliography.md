# Research bibliography

Selected external sources identified through the Cognous Stack research review completed on 6 October 2026 (Batches 01–04 and the final report). Component READMEs select the references relevant to their responsibility. This is a reading list, not an adopted specification or an implementation evidence ledger; consult [release status](release-status.md) and the [evidence index](evidence-index.md) for qualified claims.

## Source and review boundaries

- Links lead to original author, publisher or publication-repository records where verified. Private research reports and source copies are not republished or linked.
- Review coverage was selective. Citing a book or paper does not imply a cover-to-cover review, independent replication or endorsement. Books may require purchase or institutional access.
- Bibliographic identity and public locators were checked on 7 October 2026. The SSRN records for Barrett and Yang were identified through indexed publication records; direct page retrieval returned HTTP 403, so their availability was not verified by a successful fetch.
- The JCEE timeout note was identified from its public-release bibliographic metadata, but an original public URL was not verified. A similarly titled result from another publisher was not substituted.
- Drafts, working papers and practitioner guidance retain those statuses. None establishes Cognous compliance, certification, field effectiveness or production readiness.

## Selected references

- [OECD. *Agentic AI in organisations: Early insights from practitioner interviews*. OECD Artificial Intelligence Papers, No. 65 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1787/1257a26f-en). Qualitative practitioner research on bounded autonomy, oversight and organizational deployment. Review source: Batch 01.

- [OWASP GenAI Security Project. *State of Agentic AI Security and Governance*, version 2.01 (June 2026)](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/state-of-agentic-ai-security-and-governance/). Security synthesis covering agent identity, delegated permissions, tool access and containment. Review source: Batch 01.

- [John M. Willis. *Runtime Governance Body of Knowledge for Artificial Intelligence and Other Autonomous Systems — Glossary* (19 July 2026)](https://sustainablefuturetech.com/asg-wg-runtime-governance-glossary/). Discussion draft on authority, execution and evidence terminology; not an adopted standard or Cognous conformance requirement. Review source: Batch 01.

- Jonathan Chadbourne / JCEE Labs. *When a Timeout Is Not a Failure: Authority, Evidence, and Recovery in Consequential AI Execution*. Technical Note 001, public release v0.1.1 (6 October 2026). Technical note on uncertain outcomes and recovery. An original public URL has not been verified; no substitute or private copy is linked. Review source: Batch 01.

- [Siddharth Gollapudi, Nilesh Gupta, Prasann Singhal and Sewon Min. *Can Language Models Actually Retrieve In-Context? Drowning in Documents at Million Token Scale*. arXiv:2607.01538v1 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01538v1). Experimental retrieval research; useful context for testing source retrieval, not evidence of authorization correctness. Review source: Batch 02, B02-S10.

- [Alexander Barrett. *Boundary Blindness Under Artificial Intelligence: Early Cross-Industry Findings on the Missing Decision-Evidence Layer* (2026)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7210798). Working paper on carrying the basis for reliance across organizational boundaries; proposed architecture, not a validated interoperability guarantee. Review source: Batch 03, B03-S01.

- [Mick Yang et al. *AI Epistemic Risks: Emerging Mechanisms & Evidence* (2026)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6873005). Research synthesis on persuasion, cognitive offloading and feedback loops; context for evidence quality and independent judgment. Review source: Batch 03, B03-S08.

- [Chip Huyen. *Designing Machine Learning Systems*. O’Reilly (2022)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-machine-learning/9781098107956/). Engineering reference for monitoring and deployment evaluation; the research review used only the supplied second part. Review source: Batch 03, B03-S09.

- [Chip Huyen. *AI Engineering: Building Applications with Foundation Models*. O’Reilly (December 2024)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/). Engineering reference for application evaluation; the research review used a partial copy, not the full book. Review source: Batch 03, B03-S10.

- [John W. Creswell and J. David Creswell. *Research Design: Qualitative, Quantitative, and Mixed Methods Approaches*, fifth edition. SAGE (2018)](https://edge.sagepub.com/creswellrd5e). Research-methods reference for explicit questions, comparison designs and interpretation limits. Review source: Batch 04, B04-S01.

- [John Berryman and Albert Ziegler. *Prompt Engineering for LLMs: The Art and Science of Building Large Language Model–Based Applications*. O’Reilly (2024)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/prompt-engineering-for/9781098156145/). Practitioner reference for prompt design and application evaluation; not proof that a behavioral protocol is effective. Review source: Batch 04, B04-S05.
