# Private evaluator pilot — facilitator protocol

**Status:** Internal preparation; no sessions scheduled, invitations sent, or external evaluations performed.

## Before the pilot

1. Confirm the reviewer is specifically invited and understands read-only, evidence-only scope. Do not infer acceptance of confidentiality obligations.
2. Open [evaluation guide](README.md), [worksheet](evaluation-worksheet.md), [selected evidence inspection](../workstreams/pv-artifact-evidence-inspection-2026-10-10.md) and [current disposition](../verification/pv-resource-constrained-disposition-2026-10-10.md). Confirm links and frozen source identifiers.
3. Select **one** evaluator for the pilot, preferably an external reviewer experienced in enterprise runtime integration or security/governance. Do not manufacture a name, endorsement or commitment.
4. Prepare a controlled link-only screen walkthrough; no repository permissions, credentials, customer artifacts, downloads or software binaries by default.

## 60-minute run of show

- 0–10: Ask the evaluator to paraphrase private-review limitations and operational #30 HOLD.
- 10–20: Trace declaration, independent Authority Context and effect-time authorization. Ask what cannot be inferred from a model message.
- 20–35: Inspect selected matrix (34 passed / 1 characterized), archived representative expected-vs-observed records and two synthetic SQLite effects. Distinguish archived evidence from a new run.
- 35–45: Probe C0 check-to-commit race, optional C1 same-host limits, unknown timeout acknowledgment, original-effect reconciliation, absent observation and equivalent-business-intent dedupe.
- 45–55: Trace Replay, Evidence Pack and ODES. Ask what establishes provenance versus factual settlement.
- 55–60: Complete feedback worksheet, identify the single highest-consequence unanswered question and classify it as observation, inference, hypothesis or unavailable evidence.

## Stop and escalation criteria

Stop the session if asked to process real data, operate live payments, disclose private implementation, provide production grants/credentials, or share unreviewed distributions. For suspected exposure, use the private route in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md); do not paste contents into the feedback register.

## After the pilot

Write a **sanitized** record in the [feedback register](feedback-register.md), link only evidence that can be shared publicly and classify the question. Owner decides whether to create a separate bounded issue; don't expand V1 scope by default. A successful guided walkthrough does not clear public release or operational trust.
