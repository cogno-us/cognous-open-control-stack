## 2. Q6: five refusal changed-input types versus eleven resolution-input types

Decision (Q6, APPROVE correction note). The five refusal changed-input types and the eleven resolution-input types are separate, differently scoped sets. The comparison in [1.3](../s02/contracts/1.3-registry-r0.md) section 2.2 is corrected here; the original record is preserved.

### 2.1 What the original record says

1.3 section 2.2 has two table rows (lines 36 and 37 of the merged file):

- "Control Plane refusal input types, 5: `grant`, `policy`, `approval`, `evidence`, `role_mapping`", source `bounded.py` `RefusalInputVersion`.
- "ODES refusal input types, 11: manifest, authority_context, grant, policy, approval, evidence, role_mapping, identity, mandate, conflict, purpose", source ODES `execution-attempt-lifecycle/schema.json`, note "The two enumerations disagree (5 versus 11)". The same finding is repeated in the M0 decision packet ([s02/m0-decision-packet.md](../s02/m0-decision-packet.md) line 42) and in the [2.3 contract](2.3-mandatory-check-contract.md) section 8.

### 2.2 The correction

The 5 and the 11 are not two readings of one enumeration. They are two enumerations on two different structures:

| Set | Structure | Question it answers | Members | Source (verified) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Refusal changed input: `RefusalInputVersion.input_type` (Control Plane); `changed_input.input_type` (ODES) | Which input may a refusal name as changed between the expected and the observed version | `grant`, `policy`, `approval`, `evidence`, `role_mapping` | Control Plane `src/agent_control_plane/bounded.py` lines 375-378 (`class RefusalInputVersion`, `input_type: Literal[...]`) at `0a41faa9`; ODES `contracts/control-plane-journal/schema.json` lines 840-858 (`changed_input`, enum of the same 5) at `e86a25a1` |
| 11 | Resolution input: `resolution_input.input_type` (ODES); `ResolutionInput.input_type` (Control Plane) | Which input may a resolution read | `manifest`, `authority_context`, `grant`, `policy`, `approval`, `evidence`, `role_mapping`, `identity`, `mandate`, `conflict`, `purpose` | ODES `contracts/control-plane-journal/schema.json` lines 169-196 (`resolution_input`, `input_type` enum) at `e86a25a1`; Control Plane `bounded.py` lines 329-331 (`ResolutionInput.input_type: Literal[...]`) at `0a41faa9` |

Both sets are the same in the Control Plane and in ODES at the pinned revisions: the 5 match each other and the 11 match each other. The two sets differ in scope on purpose: a refusal names a changed input among the five kinds whose versions change over time and are compared at effect time; a resolution reads inputs across eleven kinds, including `manifest`, `authority_context`, `identity`, `mandate`, `conflict` and `purpose`. All five members of the narrower set are members of the wider set.

Citation note for the 1.3 row. The 1.3 row cites ODES `execution-attempt-lifecycle/schema.json`. That file exists at `e86a25a1` (178 lines) and it carries a `resolution_input` definition whose `input_type` enum is the same 11 at line 132. So the row's citation is correct for the 11 as a resolution-input enumeration, and the label "refusal input types" on that row is the defect. The authoritative position for the same 11 in the contract that defines the journal is `control-plane-journal/schema.json` lines 169-196. The `execution-attempt-lifecycle/schema.json` file was not found to carry a refusal changed-input enumeration at that revision (a search for `changed_input` in it returned no match).

### 2.3 What changes and what does not

- Corrected reading: "5 refusal changed-input types" and "11 resolution-input types" are separate, differently scoped sets. The sentence "the two enumerations disagree (5 versus 11)" is withdrawn as a finding of disagreement. It stays in 1.3 and in the other records as the historical text.
- The original 1.3 record, the M0 packet text and the 2.3 contract section 8 text are preserved unchanged. The 2.3 contract section 8 and the 2.3 manifest note "Open disagreements" item 1 already carry an earlier correction of the same kind; this note is the decision-level successor that cites the exact lines.
- No artificial reconciliation. Nobody is asked to make the 5 and the 11 equal, to widen the Control Plane's refusal set to eleven, or to narrow the resolution set to five. Options B and C in 2.3 contract section 8 (align the Control Plane to the 11; narrow ODES to the 5) are not recommended by this note because they presuppose a disagreement the sources do not show. They also are locked-component changes (F3) and are not authorized.
- Option D in 2.3 contract section 8 (an explicit data mapping from manifest input kinds to refusal input types) is not needed to reconcile the two sets. Whether such a mapping is wanted for another purpose is an owner decision and is not made here.
- The manifest's seven `input_kinds` (`authority_context`, `role_mapping`, `grant`, `identity`, `mandate`, `conflict`, `policy`; verifier `rules_journal.py:951-953` at `c0b8ba1f`, confirmed) are a third set with a third scope (what the verifier requires to have been read). It is a subset of the eleven resolution-input types and not a subset of the five.
- The Q6 decision asks for no code change. The existing [2.3 contract](2.3-mandatory-check-contract.md) section 12 item 2 ("Decide the 5-versus-11 question") is resolved per architect Q6 as a correction note (no reconciliation is to be forced); it is not thereby recorded as closed by the operator, who may still confirm or amend it.
