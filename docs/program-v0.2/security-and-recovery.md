# Security, authority and recovery review contract

S01 must map each enabled path to an owner, threat, exact negative test, independent observer and known limitation. Threats include rule replacement/downgrade, skipped evaluator, stale activation epoch, cross-tenant scope, Orchestrator privilege escalation, producer modification of acceptance criteria, unmediated tools, ambiguous acknowledgement and crash-after-commit.

No component may infer new authority from retry, receipt, evidence validity or orchestration assignment. Preserve stable effect IDs and distinct attempt IDs; after uncertainty reconcile authenticated destination state, not blindly replay. Cancellation does not erase prior commits; compensation requires new authority. Declared adapter coverage must identify both mediated and unmediated paths. Local mocks do not establish production confinement.
